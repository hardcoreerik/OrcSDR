## Presentation policy

**Supported/working** describes the implemented experience. **Measured / RF-received / physically tested** describes the source of evidence. **Interpreted** describes an optional derived or esoteric transformation. These categories are independent.

Do not call Shortwave, Airband, decoded Meshtastic, SSTV, or VDL2 broadly experimental simply because an older phase-specific handoff lacks a later device log. Also do not substitute a maintainer report for a timestamped, exact-firmware acceptance test; label the provenance explicitly. Retain legitimate limits (SSTV per-mode signal conditions, unknown airline-specific payloads, E4000 frequency bounds, LoRa encryption, sensor calibration, long-soak behavior). Records from older periods remain historically intact.

> **Dead Air is allowed to be weird. Dead Air is not allowed to lie about what it measured.**
