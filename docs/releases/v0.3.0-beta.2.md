# OrcSDR v0.3.0-beta.2 — release notes

Prepared 2026-10-02. Previous release: [`v0.3.0-beta.1`](v0.3.0-beta.1.md).

These notes say what changed, what we tested, and what we did not. They are a dated record; the current evidence table
is [`PROJECT_STATUS.md`](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/OrcSDR/blob/main/PROJECT_STATUS.md). **Status: release candidate.**
The exact-tag hardware gate in [`docs/M5BURNER_HARDWARE_GATE.md`](../M5BURNER_HARDWARE_GATE.md) has not been run on this
build yet, and this release is published only after it passes. Install, settings and troubleshooting steps are unchanged
from [beta.1](v0.3.0-beta.1.md) and [`docs/M5BURNER_RELEASE.md`](../M5BURNER_RELEASE.md); remember that an M5Burner install resets saved
settings and the settings-safe installer keeps them.

## The short version

- **A newer driver, `esp_rtl_sdr` v0.9.3.** The tuner's RF filter now follows the frequency you tune, so the Blog V3c and the
  Nooelec hear well away from FM, and the Blog V4 gains about 4 dB at 230 MHz; unplugging a dongle mid-stream or a dongle that
  fails to enumerate is handled instead of leaving the USB stack stuck. Details below.
- **An Airband dashboard:** an AM scanner with an airport catalog and receiver location, a voice band-pass, a persistent fixed
  gain, and a live SCOPE tab with spectrum and waterfall.
- **Fluid scopes:** FM, Airband, Shortwave, AM, CB and P25 spectrum views now redraw smoothly, with waterfall style chips; the FM
  view has clearer status badges, separate left and right meters, an IQ indicator and peak hold.
- **Tab5 keyboard support**, a reworked Home layout with palettes, and a filter popup.
- **The C6 radio:** firmware 2.12.6 and 3.0.6 are both accepted, and the update to 3.0.6 is optional (3.0.6 stops M5 Launcher's
  Wi-Fi/OTA from working).
- **Fixes:** a boot hang after a software restart (about one in five restarts, [#127](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/OrcSDR/issues/127))
  and a crash when unplugging a dongle while audio was playing (the speaker could be released twice).

## The driver: what is new in `esp_rtl_sdr` 0.9.3

OrcSDR now pins the published driver release **v0.9.3** (it was v0.9.1). See the driver's
[release notes](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/esp-rtl-sdr/blob/master/docs/releases/v0.9.3.md) for the detail.

- **RF mux and tracking filter follow the tuned band** (Blog V3c, Nooelec; V4 and V4L above 28.8 MHz). Before, the tuner stayed on
  the FM setting everywhere, so away from FM the stream ran but heard almost nothing. The band table comes from our own captures of
  the vendor driver. On a Tab5, 453.925 MHz went from about 17 dB to about 46 dB over noise on the Nooelec and from about 1 dB to
  40 to 45 dB on the V3c; the V4 gained about 4 dB at 230 MHz; the V4L is neutral.
- **Unplugging a dongle, a dongle that fails to enumerate, and bad control transfers** are handled: a bounded shutdown on unplug, a
  root-port power-cycle retry with back-off, and checks on control-transfer sizes.
- **Nooelec:** a manual gain no longer breaks every later start, and the low-band sample rates (225 to 300 kS/s) now start.
- The reports behind several of these came from David Coulson ([@davidcoulson](https://github.com/davidcoulson)): the missing RF
  band select (driver #25), the unplug and enumeration failures (driver #26) and the low-band rate error (driver #24). His reports
  informed our investigation; the fixes were written and verified here.

## What we tested

On a Tab5 with the owner's dongles, using development images built from the driver commits that became v0.9.3 (not yet the final
release image):

- **Band select:** Nooelec and V3c at 453.925 MHz peak 44 to 45 dB over noise with FM unchanged; V4 at 230 MHz 9.3 to 13.2 dB
  over noise with the change versus without, same dongle and antenna; the V4L streams and tunes correctly.
- **Unplug and replug:** five cycles on each of the Nooelec, V3c, V4 and V4L; every unplug was detected, re-probed and restarted,
  with no reboots and no leaked transfers. Two events are recorded honestly: on the V4 one stream start in seven replugs returned a
  USB error and the next replug recovered; on one V3c run the Wi-Fi link to the C6 failed during a replug and the Tab5 froze until
  it was reset ([#109](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/OrcSDR/issues/109) and [#110](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/OrcSDR/issues/110),
  already open for the V3). A rerun of the same V3c test passed cleanly.
- **Build:** the full ESP-IDF 5.5.4 build of OrcSDR succeeds; host tests and the documentation-truth check pass.

## What we did not test

- The final release image on hardware (the exact-tag gate above: private M5Burner install, Home, FM audio, RTL-SDR, Wi-Fi scan,
  saved-profile connection, RF24, the UI regression command and the installer package).
- Hubs, long runs of this exact build, and any dongle, antenna or site other than the owner's.
- The Airband, scope and keyboard changes on dongles other than the ones used during their development.
- The V4L's band-select change against a build without it (it was neutral earlier; this was not repeated).
