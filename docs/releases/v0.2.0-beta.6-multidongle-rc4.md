# OrcSDR v0.2.0-beta.6 Multi-Dongle RC4

> **Final publication result (2026-09-12):** the exact merged M5Burner package
> described here passed its install/boot gate and was published as immutable tag
> `v0.2.0-beta.6-multidongle-rc4`. Candidate requirements below preserve their
> pre-publication chronology.

RC4 is an experimental prerelease for receiver testing. It replaces RC3 for
multi-dongle testing and adds the hardware-verified RTL-SDR Blog V3C tuning
repair.

## Important packaging correction

An initial local RC4 package was rejected before release. It used the correct
flash layout, but rebooted before OrcSDR started because eight additional
aligned bytes of driver state left ESP-IDF unable to reserve the configured
40 KiB internal/DMA pool. The final RC4 moves that small fault-guard state into
the existing RTC-retained record and restores RC3's measured internal-memory
usage without reducing the DMA reserve used by ESP-Hosted and audio.

## Downloads

- `OrcSDR-Tab5-v0.2.0-beta.6-multidongle-rc4.bin` is the complete merged image

## What changed since RC3

- Pins `esp-rtl-sdr` commit
  `9db9244c829392e7be5f320873decbd71ce20401`.
- Corrects RTL-SDR Blog V3/V3C FM tuning by matching the tuner and RTL2832
  demodulator to the same captured 3.570 MHz intermediate frequency.
- Preserves the RTL-SDR Blog V4 tuner and demodulator path at its existing
  matched 1.814972 MHz intermediate frequency.
- Leaves Nooelec tuning unchanged because its intermediate-frequency behavior
  has not yet been proven with physical captures.
- Prevents two concurrent paths from stopping and deleting the same USB
  fault-guard timer.
- Releases driver mutexes and semaphores when installation exits early,
  including the USB safe-mode path.
- Preserves the full 40 KiB internal/DMA reserve by moving the USB fault
  guard's timer handle and current-boot flag out of ordinary internal BSS.
- Updates the driver evidence documents to separate superseded investigation
  hypotheses from the final physical tuning result.

No station-specific frequency correction or OrcSDR display workaround was
added. The correction is in the shared receiver driver where the mismatch
originated.

## Physical receiver evidence

The matched-IF firmware immediately before the final cleanup commit was tested
with a real RTL-SDR Blog V3C:

- Entering 96.1 MHz displayed 96.1 MHz and received KEZL correctly.
- Entering 99.1 MHz displayed 99.1 MHz and received The Beat with matching RDS.
- Cold start and hot retune behaved consistently.
- Unplugging and reconnecting restored the V3C profile and correct tuning.
- USB-powered and battery-powered cold boots completed successfully.

The RTL-SDR Blog V4 was then reconnected and repeated the 96.1 and 99.1 MHz
tests with correct tuning and matching RDS. Its original initialization and IF
path remain unchanged.

The final RC4 driver commit adds the USB fault-guard cleanup, startup-memory
repair, and documentation described above. The complete packaged RC4 image is
the image that must now pass the local flash regression before this candidate
is published as a GitHub release.

## Receiver status

- **RTL-SDR Blog V4:** tested non-regression baseline.
- **RTL-SDR Blog V3/V3C:** experimental; physical FM tuning, RDS, streaming,
  hotplug, and cold boot have passed on one V3C unit. Manual and automatic gain
  behavior still needs wider testing.
- **Nooelec NESDR SMArt V5:** experimental; detection and streaming are
  provisional, and repeatable reception has not yet been demonstrated.

V3/V3C and Nooelec receivers remain restricted to 24 MHz or above unless the
attached receiver reports an HF-upconverter capability. Unsupported controls
remain disabled.

## Retained RC3 improvements

RC4 retains the RC3 AM scanner repair, RF-analysis bounds check, USB transfer
drain repair, ADS-B gain control, LoRa receive improvements, connected-Wi-Fi
regression path, and M5Burner ZIP line-ending repair.

The unfinished AM/FM/shortwave station-database generators, scraped station
data, and catalog experiments are intentionally not included.

## Known limits

- Swapping receiver models while already inside the FM dashboard may require
  returning Home and reopening FM before audio restarts. Normal use with one
  receiver model is not affected.
- RTL-SDR Blog V3/V3C gain calibration remains provisional.
- Nooelec reception remains unverified.
- LoRa reception has improved, but intermittent missed traffic and long
  processing of difficult CRC-failed captures still need work.
- C6 unknown-version recovery remains separate work; RC4 does not claim that
  issue is fixed.
- Bias-T was not tested in this round.

## C6 firmware

Every RC4 release image contains the matching ESP-Hosted C6 3.0.6 firmware
inside the Tab5 application. The release validator must find the complete C6
image embedded byte-for-byte before the package is accepted.

If the internal C6 needs repair or updating, use the existing update or
reinstall option under **Settings > Firmware & Updates**.

## What testers should report

For RTL-SDR Blog V3/V3C or Nooelec NESDR SMArt V5 testing, please include:

- exact receiver model;
- installation method;
- frequency and band tested;
- antenna used;
- whether the waterfall moved;
- whether static, audio, or a known signal was received;
- unplug and reconnect behavior;
- a complete serial log from cold boot through the test or failure.

Antenna choice can affect what is received. Reception reports are more useful
when they include the antenna used.
