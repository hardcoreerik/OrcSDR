## Experiment 3: compact NXDN storage, same DSP

Original NXDN arrays have room for 20 samples/symbol. Current production always uses 10 (NXDN48 24k/2400, NXDN96 48k/4800). Introduce BasicFrontend<10>/<20> with explicit instantiations; preserve general Frontend alias. Compact unsupported geometries fail feed rather than silently changing sample rate. Exactly same active tap counts, coefficient generation, arithmetic and callbacks. Host sizeof 7064 -> 3624 per frontend, saved 3440 B each, 6880 B combined; production state_bytes 40128 -> 33248 on x86-64 actual dv_radio status. The 6880 B raw array delta is platform independent (assuming IEEE binary32), but real P4 allocator/free/fragmentation and placement require measurement; if decoders fall back to PSRAM it will reduce PSRAM instead. No new allocation or new internal statics, ring size untouched. Tested exact dibits and carrier offset for both rates, 4 DMR IQ recordings (non-target), synthetic noise and analog, chunks 1/37/512/4096, 48 comparisons; compact over-capacity rejected; general 48k NXDN48 accepted. Timings variable and no consistent CPU improvement asserted. CI also downloads the three pinned original NXDN clips and compares on both production geometries (applicable rate), with no raw payload published. Full-size frontend remains for offline validation.
