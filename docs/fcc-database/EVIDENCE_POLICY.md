# Evidence policy
1 published regulatory records (authority, dataset date, record IDs, authorization scope, status); 2 received observations (frequency, timestamp, decoder evidence, origin); 3 candidate associations (reason and conflicts); 4 independently confirmed transmitter identity (separate explicit evidence, never auto-promoted by lookup). Emission is a compatibility hint only; preserve contradictory decoder facts and competing matches; no arbitrary score as calibrated probability. No match is not evidence of illegal or unlicensed transmission. Distinguish no match, partial coverage, unavailable/failed/stale database. Coordinates are transmitter/authorized geometry only; mailing address is not transmitter location. Replay separate from live. UI uses “License candidate”, never inferred identity. Preserve evidence provenance and uncertainty.
