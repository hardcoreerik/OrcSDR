# Home and shared controls

## Home

Home reports receiver readiness, power, host connection state, and access to global Settings. It is safe to use OrcSDR without an authenticated host.

## Volume

The header shows **Home**, a **speaker** and **Settings**. Tap the speaker to open the volume tray: a large panel centred just below the status bar, drawn on top of whatever is on screen.

![The volume tray open over the Home screen](images/volume-tray.png)

- **Slider:** tap or drag along the bar to set the level; the percentage is shown on the right. Setting a level above zero, by tapping or dragging, turns sound on.
- **Speaker square:** tap it to mute or unmute. A quick second tap on the header speaker (a double tap) opens the tray without muting.
- **Closing:** tap anywhere outside the tray, or wait five seconds without touching it. Tapping Home or Settings closes the tray and goes straight to that button.
- **No accidental taps:** while the tray is open, and for a moment after it closes, the dashboard underneath ignores touches. A tap that opens, adjusts or dismisses the tray never retunes a radio or picks a list row. The dashboard also stops redrawing under the tray so scopes and waterfalls cannot paint over it.

Mute and volume are independent; setting a level above zero with the slider (tap or drag) turns sound on, and setting it to zero turns it off. Opening the tray alone never changes mute.

The old VIS button is gone. The full-screen RF visualizer is now the last entry in **ALL DASHBOARDS** (page 2).
