# Safety and privacy

OrcSDR is receive-oriented. Follow local laws, do not rely on it as a certified emergency or aviation instrument, and never transmit through hardware or software not designed and authorized for that service.

Public help media removes Wi-Fi names and addresses, exact receiver coordinates, device identifiers, credentials, and private labels. Public frequencies and public talkgroup names may remain. A `DEMO` badge always identifies deterministic injected data.
