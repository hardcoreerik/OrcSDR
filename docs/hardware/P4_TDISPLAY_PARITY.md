## 2026-11-17 (later): FT8 enabled; the real problem was internal RAM

- **Root cause of the slow Home and the unresponsive FT8 screen: internal RAM, not the decoder.** `EXT_RAM_BSS_ATTR` did nothing on the P4 console build (the Tab5 sets `CONFIG_SPIRAM_ALLOW_BSS_SEG_EXTERNAL_MEMORY=y`, the P4 console did not). Every shared screen that is linked in therefore kept its big buffers in internal RAM; registering the FT8 screen alone added about 24 KB (the FT8 runtime's 11.5 KB state, an 8 KB, a 2 KB and a 1 KB buffer), Home's raster buffer then fell back to PSRAM and ran about 16 times slower (draw 90-104 ms against 11 ms). Evidence: the last pushed commit (FT8 unregistered, so the linker dropped its data) drew Home in 11 ms; the same tree with FT8 registered drew it in 104 ms even on a fresh boot; the map showed the FT8 runtime globals in plain `.bss`. Result on a fresh boot: `dma_free` 135,935 bytes (was about 3.4 KB), Home draw 10.5 ms, median frame 86 ms, no task watchdog events.
