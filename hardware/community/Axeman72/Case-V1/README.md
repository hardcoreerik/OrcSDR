NOTE: This model is a prototype, designed solely to handily use the Tab 5 with the RTL-SDR and eliminate cable clutter. Not perfect, not final, just good enough.

Print Settings:
* Layer Height: 0.2 mm or 0.3 mm
* Perimeters/Walls: 5
* Top/Bottom Layers: 3
* Infill: 20% Gyroid
* Supports: None needed (ensure parts are oriented correctly on the print bed)

The Tab 5 brackets are slightly different from one another (sorry ;) ). Refer to the numbered assembly image for proper placement.

![Tab 5 bracket assembly](Tab5+RTLSDR%20case%20V1.png)

Screws:
* 3x12 mm self-tapping screws: 4 needed for the Tab 5 brackets
* 2x12 mm self-tapping screws: 4 needed for the RTL-SDR
* M3x8 mm screws: Optional for the Tab 5 threaded inserts

There is enough plastic material to accommodate almost any screw—just re-drill the mounting holes if needed.
