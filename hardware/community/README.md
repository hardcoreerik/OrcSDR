## Share a build

Start a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/hardcoreerik/OrcSDR/discussions) with photos or a video link and tell us what you've built. If you want the files hosted here, attach a ZIP or link to the original project and include:

- Hardware and accessories required, including the Tab5 and SDR model where applicable.
- STL/3MF files and, if available, editable CAD source.
- Print settings, assembly notes, and any known clearance, cable, power, or antenna issues.
- **The license/permission** under which OrcSDR may redistribute your design.
- Whether we may rehost your photos or video, or should simply link to your original post.

A link to an externally hosted design **does not** mean its files or media may be copied into this repository. We will only mirror files or media with an appropriate license or express permission and retain the creator's credit and original project link.

Sharing a build in Discussions doesn't grant repository write access. Maintainers decide which projects are listed or mirrored; you do not need collaborator access to submit a design. Submitting a pull request is optional, but GitHub may count authors of merged commits as code contributors.

## Licenses and safety

OrcSDR's firmware license does not automatically replace the license of a separate hardware design. Each design keeps its own stated rights and restrictions. In particular, a noncommercial hardware license is **not** permission for commercial manufacturing or sales.

Community hardware is provided by its designers without an OrcSDR hardware-compatibility guarantee. Inspect print and assembly instructions, ensure connectors and ventilation remain accessible, and follow safe power and battery practices.
