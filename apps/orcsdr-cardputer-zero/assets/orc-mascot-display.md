# Compact Orc mascot

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the user-supplied Orc
icon, then reduced with Lanczos filtering and packed into RGB565 for the LCD.
The 28 x 28 header image is embedded in src/compact/orc_mascot.hpp.

Prompt: Recreate the supplied green Orc mascot as a compact flat pixel-art UI
icon optimized for 28 x 28 RGB565 on black. Preserve the angular green head,
pointed ears, black eyebrows, white eyes and four white tusks, black mouth and
green chin. Fill the square with the face. Remove frame, circuit traces, glow,
gradients, texture and lettering. Crisp chunky lime, black and white shapes.
